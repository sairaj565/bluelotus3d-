Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026
At Blue Lotus 3D Studio, we take your workshop’s operational privacy seriously. This document outlines exactly how we handle the data you submit to our AI agent pipeline:
1

What Information We Process

We only collect the specific digital assets you actively provide to us to complete your orders. This includes: client reference photos from Pinterest/social media, custom design measurements (X, Y, Z coordinates), machine file extensions (.TAP, .NC, .GCODE) uploaded for catalog visualization, and basic contact details (WhatsApp number, name).
2

How Your Data is Used

All incoming design data, vector files, and images are processed securely inside our sandboxed cloud runtime environment powered by Google infrastructure. The files are used strictly to run our automated conversion scripts, build your G-code geometries, and render your visual showroom previews.
3

Zero Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or lease your workshop data, legacy files, or customer contact numbers to external marketing groups or manufacturing competitors. Your uploaded design folders and proprietary jobs remain strictly invisible to the public.
4

Strict Security Locks

Our cloud sandboxed environments utilize automated storage cleanup routines. Legacy G-code files processed through our reverse-engineering pipeline are securely held in an isolated environment during catalog generation and can be permanently purged upon request.

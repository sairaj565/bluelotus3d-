Machine Compatibility Guide

Which Type of .STL File Do You Need?

Every workshop has different machines and jobs. Here is a simple guide to the 3 types of files we provide so you get the exact format for your CNC router.

Primary • 85%+ of All Orders

3D Relief (Bas-Relief .STL)

For: Standard 3-Axis Flat-Bed CNC Routers

Flat back with deep organic 3D carving on the front (12mm to 18mm relief depth). Your timber board lays flat on the machine table.

  • Ideal For: Main entrance doors, pooja mandir panels, wall plaques, bed headboards, corners & borders.
  • How It Cuts: Tool moves simultaneously in X, Y, and Z to sculpt curved flower petals, faces, and plumage.
  • Compatible With: ArtCAM (all versions), Vectric Aspire, Carveco, NC Studio, Mach3, DSP.
Fast & Sharp

2.5D Vector / V-Carve Files

For: Fast Line Engraving & Flat Cutouts

Clean vector contour lines cut at sharp, controlled depths using a 60° or 90° V-groove bit or flat endmill.

  • Ideal For: Quick flat nameplate lettering, jali lattice screens, acrylic signs, and door line borders.
  • How It Cuts: Rapid cycle times with crisp, high-definition beveled borders.
  • Compatible With: Any 3-axis router (G-code .TAP / DXF).
Rotary & 3D Print

Full 360° Round 3D Models

For: 4-Axis Rotary CNC & 3D Printers

Complete, solid 3D models with geometry all the way around (front, back, and sides) for cylindrical turning.

  • Ideal For: Turned dining table legs, round temple pillars, staircase balusters, and 3D print statues.
  • How It Cuts: The 4th-axis rotary attachment spins the wood log like a lathe while the spindle carves.
  • Compatible With: Rotary 4-Axis CAM / 3D Slicers.
